- AI-assisted/original study content is permitted if factual sources and commercial-use rights are verified. Audit items individually; retain only defensible material and replace the rest with original questions from acceptable sources. Record source title/URL/access date/license/rights rationale and authoring method per item.

## Study surface v1 — 2026-10-02
- Replaced the static Study empty state with an untimed, unscored study mode.
- Learners choose a course from loaded content, then browse material grouped by
  the exact domain wording carried in each question.
- Each study item shows the question, the correct answer, and the explanation
  together, with controls to mark it as seen or needing more work.
- Study progress is coverage of the loaded material per course; it updates
  correctly when content is added, replaced, or withdrawn.
- Study marks are persisted per course through the existing SQLite store via a
  new `study_status` table and a schema v3→v4 migration.
- Study actions do not record `Attempt` rows, so they never affect practice,
  exam, review, or progress history.
- Added widget tests covering empty/partial/content-arrival cases and persistence
  tests for status storage, course isolation, and progress calculation.
- Validation: `flutter analyze` clean; `flutter test` 107 passed.
