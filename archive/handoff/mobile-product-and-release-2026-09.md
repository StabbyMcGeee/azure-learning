## Product concept and v1 commercial boundary — 2026-09-28
- Product: a native cross-platform mobile AZ-900 learning app for **iOS and Android**. Its core promise is dependable self-study anywhere: learners buy it once, download it once, and can complete the learning experience without an internet connection.
- v1 purchase model: a **single, one-time paid app purchase**. The paid install unlocks the complete v1 curriculum and all core study features permanently; there is no free tier, trial conversion flow, subscription, consumable, or paid add-on in v1.
- v1 offline contract: bundle all lesson/question content, answer explanations, local search/filter data, and learning logic in the installed app. Persist learner progress, answers, bookmarks/flags, review schedule, settings, and completion statistics locally on-device. The app must remain fully usable in airplane mode after installation.
- Internet boundary: do not require account creation, sign-in, a backend, telemetry, analytics, advertising, cloud AI, content streaming, or network access for core learning or local progress. Optional links to official Microsoft Learn documentation may open externally when the learner elects to use them; an unavailable connection must not block, erase, or degrade the core flow.
- Sync boundary: **no cloud backup or synchronisation in v1**, including no desktop-to-mobile progress transfer. A learner's progress is intentionally device-local. The app must state this plainly in onboarding/About so a device loss, reinstall, or platform change is not mistaken for a recoverable account-backed profile.
- Future monetisation is deliberately deferred: subscriptions and paid expansion packs may be considered only in a later release. Keep content, entitlement, and local-progress storage modular enough to add store-managed entitlements later, but do not surface dormant purchase UI or impose a backend/account model now.
- Consequence for the legal/store review: assess v1 as an offline, paid mobile app with locally stored progress and optional external documentation links—not as a subscription, synced service, or desktop companion. Revisit disclosures, retention/deletion/export expectations, billing design, and platform entitlements if cloud sync, accounts, subscriptions, or paid add-ons are introduced later.

## Commercial recommendation for the offline mobile v1 — 2026-09-28
- Recommended model: a free, fully offline diagnostic/sample experience plus one non-consumable, permanent “Full AZ-900 course” unlock. Use no advertising, recurring subscription, consumables, or multiple paid tiers in v1.
- Initial US reference price: $8.99 (localized store equivalents elsewhere). This positions the app as a serious, low-risk exam-prep tool while leaving room for launch discounts; $1 does not leave meaningful room for store fees, refunds, support, updates, or paid acquisition, and an ongoing subscription is poorly matched to a finite, offline exam-prep outcome.
- Free experience: allow enough bundled content to establish quality and learning fit (for example, onboarding, a diagnostic, and a representative cross-domain sample) without giving away the complete exam-prep path. The paid unlock includes the full offline question bank, explanations, exam simulation, adaptive review, and all v1 updates.
- A one-time in-app unlock is not inherently more fee-expensive than a paid download: both stores generally apply their digital-store fee to each. Apple Small Business and Google Play's first-$1M tier are commonly 15%; Google’s current June 2026 terms require checking market/install-specific fees at release. All in-app digital content sales must use Apple IAP / Google Play Billing.
- Do not claim this is the highest-profit price before evidence exists. Track aggregate storefront metrics by platform/country and price window: listing visits, downloads, unlocks, refunds, gross revenue, store fees, and net proceeds. The key comparison is 60-day net revenue per listing visitor and per installer, not sales count.
- Decision rule after enough volume: retain $8.99 if it maximises net revenue per installer with acceptable refunds; test adjacent permanent-unlock price points ($6.99 and $11.99) sequentially by matched country/cohort or release window. Do not compare raw country totals or short launch periods as if they prove price elasticity.
- Reconsider subscriptions only when the product adds durable recurring value that costs money to operate (for example, continuously refreshed curricula, cloud sync, new course tracks, tutor/community features, or a content service). Future expansion packs can be paid one-time IAPs when there is independently valuable, rights-cleared content; they are not part of v1.
